**Date:** 2026-11-01  
**Canonical risk row:** [03-risk-register.md](./03-risk-register.md) → R21 (related: R22)

## Facts (do not contradict elsewhere)

1. Task 00.1 DB safety was **attempted and rolled back**. It is **not** done and **not** mitigated.
